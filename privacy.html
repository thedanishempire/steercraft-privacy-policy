STEERCRAFT Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

STEERCRAFT respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the game handles information.

Information We Collect

STEERCRAFT does not collect, transmit, store, or share personal information. The game does not require an account or sign-in, does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and does not display third-party advertising.

Data Stored on Your Device

The game may store settings and gameplay progress locally on your device. This information remains on your device and is not sent to us.

Children’s Privacy

Because STEERCRAFT does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
