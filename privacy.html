STEERCRAFT Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how STEERCRAFT handles information when you play the game and use its optional global leaderboards.

Information We Collect

STEERCRAFT does not require you to create an account or provide your real name, email address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, or payment information.

If you choose to join the optional global leaderboards, the game uses Microsoft PlayFab to provide that feature. The following information is processed for the leaderboards:

  • a randomly generated game identifier and PlayFab player ID;
  • the display name you choose;
  • your leaderboard scores and rankings; and
  • network information, including IP address and approximate location derived from IP address.

Public Leaderboards

Your chosen display name, score, and rank are visible to other players in the global leaderboards. Please do not use your real name or include personal information in your display name.

You may change your display name in the game. Players can also hide other leaderboard names from their own device; this does not change the public leaderboard or PlayFab data.

How Information Is Used

We use leaderboard information only to provide and operate the global leaderboards, save and display scores, help prevent abuse, moderate public display names, provide support, and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

How Information Is Shared

Leaderboard information is processed by Microsoft PlayFab, our leaderboard service provider. Your display name, score, and rank are shared publicly through the in-game global leaderboards.

STEERCRAFT does not sell personal information and does not use third-party advertising.

Data Stored on Your Device

The game stores settings, gameplay progress, and any leaderboard names you choose to hide locally on your device. Removing the game may remove this local information. Leaderboard information stored by PlayFab is not removed by uninstalling the game.

Data Retention and Deletion Requests

Leaderboard information is retained while it is needed to operate the leaderboards, unless you request its deletion. To request deletion of your leaderboard data, contact us using the email address below and provide details that help us locate your record, such as your current leaderboard display name.

Children’s Privacy

STEERCRAFT does not knowingly collect directly identifying information from children. A parent or guardian with a privacy question or deletion request may contact us using the email address below.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact thedanishempire@gmail.com.
